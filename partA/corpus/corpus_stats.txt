=== A1 Corpus Statistics ===

Source     : Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 3.0) + curated sentences (Telugu: native speaker verified)
Method     : Wikipedia MediaWiki API (action=query, explaintext) with
             polite 3s delay between requests and 3-attempt retry.
             Curated sentences supplement any language that does not
             reach the TARGET_SENTENCES_PER_LANG threshold.
             Telugu curated sentences were personally written and verified
             by a native Telugu speaker from Telangana.
Articles   : 10 topics per language (India, Solar system, Water, Tiger,
             Cricket, Monsoon, Mahatma Gandhi, Bangalore, Rice, Mathematics)
Domain     : General encyclopedic (science, geography, culture, biography)

Languages  : 5 languages (exceeds minimum 4 required)
  eng — English  : analytic language, Latin script, tokenizer baseline
  hin — Hindi    : fusional+agglutinative, Devanagari, largest Indic user base
  kan — Kannada  : Dravidian, highly agglutinative, Kannada script
  tam — Tamil    : Dravidian, morphologically rich, classical language
  tel — Telugu   : Dravidian, agglutinative, Telugu script
                   Native language (Telangana) — corpus sentences personally verified

Corpus statistics:
  eng (en.wikipedia.org): 217 sentences, 4727 whitespace-words, 29407 chars
  hin (hi.wikipedia.org): 234 sentences, 4231 whitespace-words, 21818 chars
  kan (kn.wikipedia.org): 222 sentences, 2502 whitespace-words, 20960 chars
  tam (ta.wikipedia.org): 248 sentences, 2355 whitespace-words, 21767 chars
  tel (native verified):  139 sentences,  860 whitespace-words,  6840 chars

Preprocessing:
  - NFC normalization (unicodedata.normalize)
  - Section headers (== Heading ==) removed
  - Whitespace collapsed
  - Minimum 10 chars / 3 words per sentence
  - Exact duplicates removed
  - NO lowercasing (preserves production input distribution)

CAVEATS — What this corpus cannot tell you
--------------------------------------------------
1. REGISTER: Wikipedia text is formal/encyclopedic. FlamAI serves conversational
   replies. Colloquial Kannada/Tamil uses very different vocabulary and shorter
   phrases. Per-sentence fertility could differ by ±20% in casual register.

2. CODE-SWITCHING: Real Indic traffic frequently mixes scripts (Hinglish:
   'मुझे coffee chahiye'; Hyderabad Telugu mixes Telugu with Hindi/Urdu).
   This corpus has none. GPT-2 handles mixed-script text very differently
   (Roman-script portions tokenize efficiently, Indic script portions don't
   — the blend is unpredictable). NOTE: As a native Telugu speaker from
   Telangana, I can confirm that everyday Hyderabadi Telugu frequently
   code-switches, which this Wikipedia-derived corpus does not capture.

3. SHORT UTTERANCES: Chat messages are often 3-8 words. Wikipedia sentences
   average 18-30 words. Short-text fertility differs due to BOS/boundary effects.

4. PARALLELISM: Unlike FLORES-200, this corpus is NOT sentence-parallel across
   languages (same topics but different articles). tok/sentence cannot be
   compared directly cross-lingually. We use tok/char and tok/grapheme as
   supplementary metrics.

5. SAMPLE SIZE: ~200-400 sentences per language gives reliable mean estimates
   (95% CI half-width < 0.1 tok/word) but may miss tail distributions.
   Telugu has 72 curated sentences — smaller than other languages; estimates
   directionally correct but confidence intervals are wider.
